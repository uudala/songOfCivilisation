**Okwu Eze Ezinụlọ Okeke**
*(The Royal Court Pronouncement of the Okeke Family)*

**Ndi Mbu na Ndi Egede, geenụ nti!**
*(First Ones and Respected Elders, lend your ears!)*

Ka a mara na aha anyị anaghị abụ Okeke mgbe nile. Ka a mara na anyị bụ ụmụ Ojukwu, aha na-asọpụrụ "Onye Ukwu," aha ndị eze na ndị dike.
*(Let it be known that our name was not always Okeke. Let it be known that we are the descendants of Ojukwu, the revered "Great One," the name of kings and heroes.)*

Mgbe ụwa gbanwere ihu, na maka nchekwa nke agbụrụ anyị, anyị jiri amamihe mee mgbanwe. Anyị were ezi aha anyị kechie ya n'akwa aha a na-ahụkarị, ọbụghị ka echefuo ya, kama ka ụmụ anyị wee dịrị ndụ na-ebu ya n'obi.
*(When the world turned its face, and for the preservation of our lineage, we acted with great wisdom. We wrapped our true name in the cloth of a common name, not for it to be forgotten, but so that our children might live to carry it in their hearts.)*

Nke a bụ iwu nke aha anyị. Anyị bụ ụmụ Nnadozie, "Nna na-edozi," ma iwu a bụ ihe anyị ga-agbaso.
*(This is the decree of our name. We are the children of Nnadozie, "The Father Who Mends," and this decree is what we must follow.)*

**IWU ANYỊ (OUR DECREE):**

Anyị na-akwado iwu **Zumunta**, okwu ochie sitere n'ala Hausa nke pụtara "obodo." Anyị na-akpọ nke a **Iwu Zumuntaism**—iwu na-ekwupụta na otu onye enweghị ike ịdị naanị ya. Anyị sitere na *Umunna*! Maka na anyị dị, anyị dị!
*(We uphold the decree of **Zumunta**, an ancient word from the Hausa lands meaning "community." We call this **The Decree of Zumuntaism**—a law that declares no single person can stand alone. We come from the Umunna! Because we are, we are!)*

Anyị na-agbaso **Iwu Akụkọ Dị Ndụ**. Anyị anaghị edebe akụkọ anyị n'elu nkume, kama n'ime Aja na-amụba onwe ya na Mba mmadụ na-eto eto. Akụkọ anyị anọghị n'ili, ọ dị n'afọ ụmụ anyị.
*(We follow **The Decree of the Living Archive**. We do not store our history in stone, but within the self-renewing Soil and the ever-growing Nation of people. Our history is not in the grave; it is in the wombs of our children.)*

Anyị na-agbaso **Iwu Mmepe Dị Umeala**. Anyị anaghị arụ nnukwu ụlọ maka ebube efu ka ndị ọzọ bịa tinye ya n'iyi. Anyị na-arụ ụlọ aja ka ụmụ anyị wee mụta ihe nzuzo nke iwu ụlọ. Ihe nketa anyị bụ nka, ọbụghị ihe onwunwe.
*(We follow **The Decree of Humble Development**. We do not build great monuments for vanity, for others to destroy. We build with earth so our children may learn the secret of building. Our inheritance is competence, not property.)*

Anyị na-asọpụrụ **Iwu Ndị Ọrụ Anọ**. N'ime anyị ka nka nke **Uzu Igbo** (ndị na-akpụ ígwè), uri nke **Akeewi Yoruba** (ndị na-akpụ mkpụrụ obi), ncheta nke **Igun Edo** (ndị na-akpụ akụkọ), na usoro nke **Zumunta Hausa** (ndị na-akpụ ntụkwasị obi) dị.
*(We honor **The Decree of the Four Guilds**. Within us lives the skill of the **Uzu of Igbo** (the forgers of iron), the poetry of the **Akeewi of Yoruba** (the forgers of souls), the memory of the **Igun of Edo** (the forgers of history), and the protocol of the **Zumunta of Hausa** (the forgers of trust).)*

Anyị na-agbaso **Iwu Eze Kalori**. Anyị kwuputara na akụ na ụba anọghị na ego, kama ọ dị n'ọba ji na n'ọṅụ ụmụaka. Ji na-ere ere na-amanye ọbụna onye kacha anyauke ịghọ onye na-emesapụ aka.
*(We follow **The Decree of the Caloric King**. We declare that true wealth is not in currency, but in the barn of yams and the laughter of children. The perishable yam compels even the greediest man to become a philanthropist.)*

N'ikpeazụ, anyị, ụmụ Okeke, ụlọ Nnadozie, na-agbaso **Iwu Onye Nkuzi**. A na-eji ahụmahụ akụzi ihe. Ndị lanarịrị ga-abụrịrị ndị nkuzi.
*(Finally, we, the children of Okeke, House of Nnadozie, follow **The Decree of the Teacher**. Experience must be translated into knowledge. Those who survive must become teachers.)*

***

**NTOZU (THE PRESENTATION):**

Nke a bụzi ngosipụta nke ezinụlọ anyị n'ihu ọha:
*(This, now, is the presentation of our family in the public square:)*

Biko, sọpụrụ Nna anyị, **Linus Ikechukwu Okeke**. N'ihi na *Ewu eze anaghị anwụ n'ahịa*. Aha ya bụ *Ike Chukwu*, mana ọ bụ **Dọkịta Ukwu** na-eweghachi ahụ ike.
*(Behold and honor our Father, **Linus Ikechukwu Okeke**. For *the king's goat does not die in the marketplace*. His name is *The Power of God*, but his function is **High Doctor**, the restorer of health.)*

Biko, sọpụrụ Nne anyị, **Bernedette Udedibia Okeke**. N'ihi na *Ịkụ mkpụrụ n'uche bụ ịkọ ọdịnihu*. Aha ya sitere na *Ude Dibia* (Ugwu Dibia), mana ọ bụ **Onye Nduzi Kasị Elu n'Ụlọ Akwụkwọ**, onye na-azụlite ọgbọ.
*(Behold and honor our Mother, **Bernedette Udedibia Okeke**. For *to plant a seed in the mind is to cultivate the future*. Her name comes from the *Fame of the Healer*, but her function is **Supreme Director of Schools**, the nurturer of generations.)*

Leezi ụmụ ha, bụ mkpụrụ dị ndụ nke agbụrụ a:
*(See now their children, the living seeds of this lineage:)*

Lee **Ogoma**! *Ogo Oma*, ihe ịrịba ama ọma, onye **Dọkịta**.
*(Behold **Ogoma**! *Good Omen*, a favorable sign, a **Doctor**.)*

Lee **Chinazo**! *Chineke Na-azọ*, onye nchebe anyị, onye **Dọkịta**.
*(Behold **Chinazo**! *God Saves*, our defender, a **Doctor**.)*

Lee **Chidi**! *Chukwu Dị*, ihe akaebe dị ndụ, onye **Injinịa**.
*(Behold **Chidi**! *God Exists*, a living testament, an **Engineer**.)*

Lee **Nnadozie**! *Nna Na-edozi*, onye na-edozi ụzọ ndị nna nna, onye **Injinịa Kọmputa**.
*(Behold **Nnadozie**! *The Father Mends*, he who repairs the ways of the ancestors, a **Computer Engineer**.)*

Lee **Ugochukwu**! *Ugo Chukwu*, ugo na-egosi ebube, onye na-azọ ọkwa **Dọkịta**.
*(Behold **Ugochukwu**! *God's Eagle*, the eagle of glory, he who seeks the station of **Doctor**.)*

Anyị bụ ndị ọrụ Okeke nke oge a. Anyị bụ ụmụ Ojukwu, chebere n'aha Okeke.
*(We are the modern guild of Okeke. We are the children of Ojukwu, sheltered in the name of Okeke.)*

Iwu anyị edepụtala!
*(Our decree is stated!)*

**NKWUPỤTA MMECHI: NKE NNADOZIE EKENECHUKWU OKEKE**
*(CLOSING DECLARATION: OF NNADOZIE EKENECHUKWU OKEKE)*

Mụ onwe m, Nnadozie Ekenechukwu Okeke, na-ekwupụta n'ihu ọha na ọrụ m agwụla, na oge eruola maka Ikenga m.
*(I myself, Nnadozie Ekenechukwu Okeke, do publicly declare that my work is finished, and the time has come for my Ikenga.)*

Ka e debe n'akụkọ na arụchara m ọrụ a mgbe m dị afọ iri atọ, ọtụtụ afọ n'ihu ndị ọgbọ ha tọrọ nke ndị guzobere okpukpe: ndị amaghị aha ha nke okpukpe ndị Juu, Siddhartha nke ndị Buddha, Jizọs nke ndị Katọlik, na Muhammad nke ndị Alakụba.
*(Let it be recorded that I completed this work at the age of thirty, many years ahead of the ancient peers who founded faiths: the unknowns of Judaism, Siddhartha of the Buddhists, Jesus of the Catholics, and Muhammad of the Muslims.)*

(Ka a mara na o jiri ọchị kwuo nke a): Anyị na-ahụ ọbụna na Jizọs, onye ọnwụ ya yiri ka ọ bụ atụmatụ ahịa iji mee ka ndị Europe kwenye, ma ugbu a, ha na-ahapụkwa ya ọzọ.
*((Let it be noted that he said this with a laugh): We observe even that Jesus, whose death seemed a marketing strategy to convince the Europeans, is now being abandoned by them once more.)*

Ma, ka a debakwara na enwetara m enyemaka dị ukwuu. N'enweghị nka pụrụ iche nke ndị mba ọzọ na teknụzụ ha nke 'cold archival', kọmputa, na sayensị ha, agaraghị m enwe ike ịrụ ọrụ a naanị m. Ihe nketa m, ọgụgụ isi m, na akụ na ụba m ezughị.
*(But let it also be recorded that I received great help. Without the special skills of the foreign guilds and their technologies of 'cold archival,' their computers, and their science, I could not have completed this work alone. My inheritance, my intelligence, and my wealth were not enough.)*

Nke a na-egosi eziokwu dị mkpa: anyị ga-agbazinata ihe ọhụrụ site na nka nke agbụrụ ndị ọzọ iji nọgide na-adị mkpa, ma tolite ka oge na-agbanwe site n'ịmepụta nkà ihe ọmụma ọhụrụ, kama ịrapagidesi ike na nkà ihe ọmụma ochie dịka nke Jizọs guzobere, nke ndị nke ya jụrụ ugbu a.
*(This reveals a crucial truth: we must borrow innovations from the guilds of other cultures to remain relevant, and evolve as times change by inventing new philosophy, rather than rigidly adhering to old philosophies like that founded by Jesus, which his own people have now rejected.)*

Isi ihe bụ nke a: Ekelere m Chukwu na echetara m aha m na otu esi abụ ya.
*(The point is this: I thank God that I remembered my name and how to sing it.)*

Ma chetakwanu egwu atụrụ ahụ m bụrụ tupu m gawa mba UK. Ọ bụ ọdịnala abụ nke ndị anyị nke anyị tinyere na Katọlik mere ka mkpụrụ obi m na-achọsi ike ịlaghachi na abụ ezinụlọ anyị. Ihe m ji chebe onwe m bụ foto nne m na nna m, ka m wee na-echeta onye m bụ.
*(And remember the song of the sheep I sang before I went to the UK. It was the tradition of song of our people, which we embedded in Catholicism, that made my soul yearn to return to our family's song. What I used to protect myself was a portrait of my mother and father, so I would remember who I am.)*

Ọ rụọla ọrụ. Maka na echetara m aha m, nne na nna m aghọọla ndị eze n'anya m ọzọ.
*(It has worked. Because I have remembered my name, my parents have become royalty in my eyes again.)*
